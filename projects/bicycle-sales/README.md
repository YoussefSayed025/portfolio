# Bicycle shop sales dashboard

Power BI dashboard tracking units sold, revenue, profit and product performance by country, customer and segment for 2023-2024. Japan was the top market ($18.4M) and Paseo the leading product ($33.0M).

## Business questions
- Which products, countries and customer segments drive sales and profit?
- How do sales trend month over month across 2023-2024?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?

## Tools & methodology
Power BI (single-page report "Sales Overview", 20 visuals, custom sunburst visual, DAX measures on top of one flat table) with the dataset prepared in Excel.

## Key findings
- $118.73M total sales, $16.89M profit (14.23% margin), 1.13M units sold.
- Japan is the top market at $18.4M; Paseo is the top product at $33.0M.
- Government is the largest segment ($52.5M), followed by Small Business ($42.4M).
