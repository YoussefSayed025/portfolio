# Coffee sales analysis & forecasting

Power BI dashboard covering sales, discount impact and product-level performance for 2023-2024, with a forecast. 701K in total sales; Colombian and Costa Rican coffees led revenue.

## Business questions
- Which coffees drive the most revenue?
- How do discounts affect sales?
- What does the sales forecast show for the coming months?

## Tools & methodology
Power BI (single-page report "Sales Analysis And Forecasting Dashboard" with DAX measures for month-over-month comparisons and a built-in forecast), SQL for data preparation, and a companion presentation deck.

## Key findings
- 701K total sales; Colombian and Costa Rican coffees led revenue.
- Discounts were applied on just over half of sales (50.7%).
- The forecast panel projects sales beyond October 2024.
